Pre-check confidence is an estimate of the share of strings a native speaker would accept unchanged. It comes from a mechanical check of every string and a read of a sample of 160 strings by an AI checker, reduced for what the checker cannot see in that language. It is not a review: a language stays beta until native speakers have approved it, and strings that are new or changed are approved separately. Red is 50 % or less, green above 95 %.

Native speakers check the interface text of their language here. A reviewer answers every string with OK, CHANGE (and a proposal) or UNCLEAR, then completes the review; an Administrator or StCd Owner decides on the proposals, and an accepted proposal is shown by the application at once. How this fits together is in docs/26-translations.md.
